artwork are covered by the repository `LICENSE`.

The photographic wallpapers in this local installation are reference images
from third-party websites. They are retained for personal desktop use and are
**not licensed by this project for redistribution**. They must not be included
in a public release without written permission from the relevant rights holder.

The public release ships no wallpaper images. Users may place personally
obtained images in:

```text

```

user-supplied devotional text. Any rights in that text remain with their
